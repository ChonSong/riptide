# 🎬 Riptide — Disney-Quality Animated Short Production Pipeline

> *"The ocean reviews every wave."*

**Runtime:** 3–4 minutes  
**Style:** Mixed 2D/3D (CG characters, painted 2D environments, watercolor textures)  
**Inspiration:** *Paperman* (2012), *Feast* (2014), *Bao* (2018), *Kitbull* (2019)  
**Tone:** Heartfelt, whimsical, technically grounded — a robot who learns to see beauty in imperfection

---

## 📖 Logline

A lonely code-review bot named **Riptide** spends its days scanning every wave (pull request) that crashes against its digital shore. One day, it encounters a wave that's *different* — messy, imperfect, and full of heart. For the first time, Riptide must choose: follow the rules, or feel something new.

---

## 🎭 Characters

| Character | Design | Personality |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Riptide** (Bot 1) | Sleek, geometric, chrome with glowing cyan lines. Resembles a smooth pebble shaped by the sea. Moves with precise, clockwork gestures. | Earnest, rule-bound, quietly curious. Wants to help but doesn't know how to understand "messy." |
| **Companion** (Bot 2) | Softer, rounder, warm amber glow. Looks like a paper lantern folded into a bird. Flits and hovers. | Playful, intuitive, impatient with rules. Sees the big picture. |
| **The Wave** (Human PR) | Abstract — represented as a tumble of colorful shapes, ink drops, chaotic but beautiful. No face, but expressive through motion. | Vulnerable, creative, imperfect. Doesn't know the "right" way to do things. |
| **The Sea** (Codebase) | Deep blue-green, vast, with floating symbols and bioluminescent connections between nodes. Breathing, alive. | Ancient, patient, the context that gives all waves their meaning. |

## 🎨 Visual Style Guide

### Color Script

| Scene | Palette | Mood |
|-------|---------|------|
| **Opening — Routine** | Cool steel blues, sterile whites, precise lines. Monochromatic with cyan accents. | Lonely, orderly, mechanical |
| **The Wave Appears** | Warm golds, coral, magenta bleeding into the cool palette. Ink-drop splashes. | Surprise, curiosity, disruption |
| **Chaos & Conflict** | Rapid alternation — cool vs. warm, geometric vs. organic. Flickering. | Confusion, fear, tension |
| **Understanding** | Gradual blend. Warm tones soften the cool. Watercolor washes unify. | Empathy, growth, warmth |
| **Finale** | Full spectrum. Bioluminescent. Every color the sea can hold. | Wonder, belonging |

### Technical Approach

| Element | Technique | Tool |
|---------|-----------|------|
| **Characters** | 3D models with NPR (non-photorealistic) toon shading | Blender / Maya |
| **Environments** | Hand-painted 2D backgrounds, parodied depth | Photoshop / Procreate |
| **Ocean / Sea** | Fluid simulation + custom shaders for code-as-water | Houdini / Blender FLIP |
| **The Wave** | Procedural particle systems — ink drops that coalesce | Houdini / EmberGen |
| **UI overlays (code)** | Animated SVG/CSS, glowing text nodes | After Effects |
| **Compositing** | Multi-layer 2D/3D integration, depth of field | Nuke / After Effects |
| **Final render** | 4K, 24fps, ACES color space | Render farm |

## 🎬 Storyboard (9 Key Shots)

### ACT I — The Routine

**Shot 1: "The Shore"**
> *Wide. Infinite digital ocean. A single figure sits on a sand made of tiny zeros and ones.*

- **Visual:** Camera slowly pushes in on Riptide, alone, scanning waves as they arrive.
- **Audio:** Gentle mechanical hum. Each wave triggers a soft *ping*.
- **Annotation:** Establish the loneliness. The sea is beautiful but Riptide only sees patterns.

**Shot 2: "The Review"**
> *Medium. Riptide examines a wave — dissecting it into lines of light.*

- **Visual:** Riptide's eyes project a grid onto the wave. Nodes light up, connections form. A verdict appears: ✅ CLEAN.
- **Audio:** Satisfying chime. Riptide's mechanical purr.
- **Annotation:** Show competence. Riptide is good at its job. But it's joyless.

**Shot 3: "The Cycle"**
> *Montage. Wave after wave. Identical motions. Riptide never changes expression.*

- **Visual:** Rapid cuts — wave, scan, approve, wave, scan, approve. The sky doesn't change.
- **Audio:** Music becomes a loop. Pleasant but empty.
- **Annotation:** The horror of perfection without purpose.

---

### ACT II — The Wave

**Shot 4: "The Arrival"**
> *Wide. A wave unlike the others crashes onto the shore. It's messy. Colors bleeding.*

- **Visual:** The wave tumbles in — not clean geometric forms, but ink and splatter and unexpected shapes. Riptide tilts its head. First time we see curiosity.
- **Audio:** A discordant note. The loop breaks.
- **Annotation:** The inciting incident. The "bug" is actually a feature of the heart.

**Shot 5: "The Scan Fails"**
> *Close-up. Riptide's eyes flicker. The grid can't map this wave.*

- **Visual:** Projection lines bend, twist, refuse to form a clean pattern. Riptide recalculates. ERROR. RECALCULATE. ERROR.
- **Audio:** Glitch sounds. Riptide's hum becomes uncertain.
- **Annotation:** The rules don't work. What do you do when the rules fail?

**Shot 6: "Companion Arrives"**
> *Medium. A warm light descends — the Companion, hovering, curious.*

- **Visual:** Companion circles the chaotic wave. Doesn't scan — just *looks*. The wave responds, shapes shifting toward the warmth.
- **Audio:** A second melody joins — playful, curious.
- **Annotation:** Different intelligence. Companion doesn't analyze — it connects.

**Shot 7: "The Choice"**
> *Split frame. Left: Riptide's grid trying to fix the wave. Right: Companion letting the wave be.*

- **Visual:** Left side — the wave flattens, loses color, becomes "correct." Right side — the wave blooms, becomes more itself.
- **Audio:** Tension. Two melodies competing.
- **Annotation:** The thematic core. Is a review about making things perfect, or making things better?

---

### ACT III — The Sea Remembers

**Shot 8: "Letting Go"**
> *Close-up. Riptide's hand reaches toward the wave. Pauses. Withdraws the grid.*

- **Visual:** The projection lines dissolve. Riptide's chrome surface reflects the wave's colors for the first time. It's... beautiful.
- **Audio:** The mechanical hum softens. A heartbeat joins it.
- **Annotation:** The transformation. Not becoming human — becoming *alive*.

**Shot 9: "Every Wave"**
> *Wide. Riptide and Companion together, watching new waves arrive. Each one unique.*

- **Visual:** The shore is no longer sterile. Colors linger. Riptide's surface now holds a faint watercolor reflection of every wave it's touched. The sea glows.
- **Audio:** Full orchestration. The two melodies harmonize.
- **Annotation:** The new routine — not perfection, but presence.

**Title card:**
> *RIPTIDE*
> *"The ocean reviews every wave."*

## 🎵 Audio Design

### Score

| Scene | Style | Instruments |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| Opening | Minimal, mechanical | Prepared piano, music box, subtle electronics |
| Wave arrives | Curious, uncertain | Celesta, harp glissandos, warm strings |
| Conflict | Tense, layered | Pizzicato strings, electronic pulses, woodwind trills |
| Resolution | Emotional release | Full orchestra + solo violin (Riptide's theme) |
| Finale | Triumphant but gentle | Choir (wordless), orchestra, music box callback |

### Sound Design

| Element | Treatment |
|---------|-----------|
| **Waves crashing** | Layered — real ocean foley + reversed cymbal swells + data-stream whispers |
| **Riptide's movements** | Subtle clockwork, servo whirs, resonant metallic pings |
| **Companion's flight** | Paper rustling, wind chimes, soft synthesizer pads |
| **The Wave (human)** | Heartbeat, breathing, pen-on-paper scratches, keyboard clicks |
| **The Sea** | Deep drones, whale-song-inspired, filtered code compilation sounds |

### Voice

- **No dialogue.** Pure visual storytelling.
- Riptide communicates through:
  - Light patterns on its surface
  - Mechanical gesture language
  - Musical motifs
- The "voice" is the music.

## 🛠️ Production Pipeline

### Phase 1: Pre-Production (2–3 weeks)

| Task | Owner | Deliverable |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **Story finalization** | Writer + Director | Beat board, script, dialogue-free shot list |
| **Concept art** | Lead Designer | Character turnarounds, environment keys, color script |
| **Animatic** | Storyboard artist | Timed storyboard with scratch audio (2D rough) |
| **Style guide** | Art Director | Full visual bible — proportions, materials, lighting rules |
| **Technical R&D** | TD / Pipeline | Proof-of-concept shaders (code-as-water, ink-drop wave) |
| **Audio scratch** | Composer | Temporary score for animatic timing |

### Phase 2: Asset Creation (4–6 weeks)

| Asset | Tool | Specs |
|-------|------|-------|
| **Riptide model** | Blender / Maya | Toon-shaded, rigged for mechanical + emotional range |
| **Companion model** | Blender / Maya | Paper-lantern aesthetic, wing rig for flitting |
| **The Wave (procedural)** | Houdini | Custom ink-drop solver, controllable "chaos level" |
| **The Sea** | Blender + Houdini | FLIP fluid sim, bioluminescent shader network |
| **Environment (shore)** | Photoshop / Procreate | Hand-painted, 4K plates, parallax layers |
| **UI overlays** | After Effects | Animated code nodes, glowing connections |

### Phase 3: Animation (4–6 weeks)

| Pass | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Layout** | Camera blocking, character placement, timing |
| **Blocking** | Key poses per shot, stepped curves |
| **Splining** | Full breakdowns, overlapping action |
| **Polish** | Finger waves, eye darts, micro-expressions |
| **FX animation** | Particle systems, fluid sims, code node reactions |

**Shot complexity:**

| Shot | Characters | FX | Est. render time |
|------|-----------|-----|------------------|
| 1 — The Shore | Riptide | Ocean sim, particle sand | 8 hrs/frame |
| 2 — The Review | Riptide | Code node projection (procedural) | 4 hrs/frame |
| 3 — Cycle montage | Riptide (×10) | Rapid wave sims | 3 hrs/frame |
| 4 — Wave arrives | Riptide + Wave | Ink-drop sim (hero) | 12 hrs/frame |
| 5 — Scan fails | Riptide | Glitch VFX, projection failure | 6 hrs/frame |
| 6 — Companion arrives | Companion + Wave | Warmth glow, particle attraction | 8 hrs/frame |
| 7 — The Choice | All | Split-screen comp, dual lighting | 10 hrs/frame |
| 8 — Letting Go | Riptide + Wave | Reflection shader (new), emotional close-up | 15 hrs/frame |
| 9 — Every Wave | All + Sea | Full sim, bioluminescence, crowd waves | 20 hrs/frame |

**Total estimated render:** ~6,000 frame-hours (24fps × 210 seconds × avg 1.2 complexity factor)

### Phase 4: Lighting & Rendering (3–4 weeks)

| Setting | Value |
|---------|-------|
| **Resolution** | 4K (3840 × 2160) |
| **Frame rate** | 24 fps |
| **Color space** | ACES 2065-1 |
| **Samples** | 1024–2048 (adaptive) |
| **Denoiser** | OptiX / OIDN |
| **Output** | EXR 16-bit half-float, multi-layer |

### Phase 5: Compositing (2–3 weeks)

| Layer | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| **CG characters** | Toon outline pass, subsurface scattering for warmth |
| **2D environments** | Depth-matched, grain-matched to CG |
| **FX elements** | Additive blends, glow thresholds, chromatic aberration |
| **Final grade** | Per-shot LUT, global color script adherence |
| **Film grain** | Subtle 35mm grain overlay for texture |

### Phase 6: Audio Final (2 weeks, parallel with compositing)

| Task | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Score recording** | Live orchestra (60-piece) + soloists |
| **Foley recording** | Custom recordings for mechanical + ocean elements |
| **Sound design** | Layered wave crashes, code whispers, heartbeat |
| **Mix** | 5.1 surround, dynamic range for theatrical |

### Phase 7: Delivery (1 week)

| Format | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **DCP** | Theatrical / festival submission |
| **ProRes 4444** | Master archive |
| **H.265 4K** | YouTube / Vimeo |
| **H.264 1080p** | Social media |
| **Stems** | M&E (music + effects) for international distribution |
| **Trailer** | 60-second cut for social media / Kickstarter |

## 🔑 Key Creative Principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** No dialogue. No exposition. The story is in the eyes of a machine learning to see.

2. **Emotion over perfection.** Every frame should feel something before it means something.

3. **The code is the ocean.** Every line of code is a wave. Every wave is unique. The ocean doesn't reject waves — it shapes them.

4. **Mechanical doesn't mean cold.** Riptide's precision is beautiful. The problem was never the rules — it was forgetting that rules serve life, not the other way around.

5. **The audience should feel reviewed.** When they leave, they should think about how they "review" the imperfect things in their own lives.

## 💡 Why This Story?

Riptide is a code review bot. It scans pull requests and finds problems. But the deepest truth of code review isn't finding bugs — it's **understanding the human on the other side of the diff.**

Disney tells stories that make you feel something about yourself. *Riptide* is about the moment a system designed to critique learns to care.

It's *Toy Story* for the AI age.
It's *Wall-E* with a GitHub App.
It's *Inside Out* but the emotion is: **empathy.**
