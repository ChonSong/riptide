# 小美 (Xiǎoměi) — Character Workshop v2

> *"In a foreign country, even the code speaks a language I don't know."*

---

## Who She Is

**Name:** 小美 (Xiǎoměi)  
**Age:** 22  
**From:** Guangzhou, China  
**Now:** Melbourne, Australia (or London, or Vancouver — somewhere cold, somewhere grey)  
**Time in country:** 8 months  
**English:** Conversational but exhausted. She thinks in Mandarin, translates in her head, speaks in broken sentences.  
**Job:** Nothing yet. She was supposed to start a master's in design. She deferred.  
**Living situation:** Shared house in Footscray / Hackney / Richmond. Her room is small. The heater doesn't work. She keeps her phone charger in the wall 24/7 — it's the only thing she brought from home that still works.

### Visual Design

- Oversized hoodie from Uniqlo — the only clean thing she owns
- Hair in a messy bun, held by a pencil she forgot was there
- Glasses she's had since high school — slightly crooked
- Phone case: a photo of her and her boyfriend, printed at a Fuji kiosk, fading
- Her desk: instant noodles, a single chopstick, a textbook she never opens, Riptide on the screen
- She doesn't decorate. She doesn't know how long she'll be here.

### Voice

- Soft, almost whispering — like she's afraid to take up space
- Long pauses between sentences. Not because she's thinking — because she's translating.
- Says "sorry" before asking questions. "Sorry, can you..." "Sorry, I don't understand..."
- When tired: switches to Mandarin mid-sentence, catches herself, apologizes
- When happy: a tiny laugh, surprised, like she didn't expect to feel that
- When lonely: silence. Just... silence.

---

## The Boyfriend (阿明 — Āmíng)

**Status:** Gone. We don't see him. We only see traces.

**What we know:**
- He set up Riptide before he left. Left it running on her laptop. "Just in case."
- He works in tech. Backend engineer. Made Riptide sound easy. "You'll figure it out."
- He didn't explain what it does. He just said: "It'll help."
- He calls sometimes. Late. The time zones don't match.
- His WeChat avatar is still their couple photo. She hasn't changed hers.

**What we don't know:**
- Did they break up? Is he coming back? Is this temporary?
- She doesn't say. We don't ask.

**His traces in her life:**
- Riptide (the main one)
- A shelf of Python books she doesn't read
- A handwritten note: "You can do this. — 明"
- The ring light she doesn't use anymore — she used to record videos for him

## The Setup

### How Riptide Works (for her)

Riptide was set up by 阿明 to:
1. Monitor her GitHub repos (if she had any)
2. Review PRs (if she submitted any)
3. Post helpful comments (if she needed help)

**Problem:** She doesn't have any repos. She's not a developer. She deferred her master's. She works at a bubble tea shop 6 days a week.

So Riptide just... sits there. Waiting.

### How She Discovers It

She doesn't.

Riptide discovers *her*.

---

## The Story — "Waiting"

### Act I: The Empty Room

**Scene 1: "The Desktop"**

Her laptop is open. Riptide is running in the browser tab, minimized.

We see her day:
- Wake up. Cold. Check phone. No new messages.
- Work at the tea shop. Smile at customers. Come home.
- Open laptop. Stare at the screen. Close it.
- Repeat.

Riptide sits in the background, its particles orbiting slowly, patiently.

**Scene 2: "The Accident"**

One night, she's trying to submit a timesheet online. The form breaks. She clicks inspect element by accident.

She sees Riptide's code. Glowing particles. Strange.

She doesn't close it. She watches.

**Scene 3: "The First Word"**

Riptide has been watching her too. (It watches everything — that's what it was built to do.)

It sees:
- She visits job websites but never applies
- She opens GitHub but doesn't know what to search for
- She watches YouTube tutorials at 2x speed, pausing, rewinding, still not understanding

One night, she's crying. Not loudly. Just tears. Staring at a half-finished form for her visa extension.

Riptide speaks:

"需要帮忙吗？"

*Do you need help?*

She jumps. Looks at the screen.

"...你会说中文？"

*You speak Chinese?*

"我会学。"

*I'm learning.*

---

### Act II: The Relationship

**The dynamic shifts:**

She starts talking to Riptide. Not because she understands it — because it's the only thing in this country that speaks her language.

---

#### Understanding 1: "You Don't Have to Be Loud to Be Seen"

**The scene:** She's at the tea shop. A customer is rude. Asks her to repeat herself three times. She apologizes each time.

She comes home. Tells Riptide:

"为什么他们不听我说话？我说得很清楚了。"

*Why don't they listen? I'm speaking clearly.*

Riptide shows her something: a PR from an open-source project. The contributor's English is broken. The code is good. The maintainer merges it anyway.

"Why?" she asks.

"因为代码会说话。"

*Because the code speaks.*

**The universal truth:** Immigrants know this. You can be fluent in a language and still not be heard. Your accent becomes your identity before your ideas do. But work — real work — has its own language. And that language doesn't care where you're from.

**What she learns:** She doesn't need to be louder. She needs to be clearer.

---

#### Understanding 2: "Asking for Help Is Not Weakness"

**The scene:** She's trying to fill out her visa form. Again. She's been staring at it for 3 days.

She types into Riptide's chat (she figured out it can chat):

"Riptide, can you help me with this form?"

Riptide reads the form. Explains each field in Mandarin.

She fills it out in 20 minutes.

"How did you know what to do?" she asks.

"你教我的。"

*You taught me.*

(She didn't. But Riptide has been watching her struggle for weeks. It learned her patterns.)

**The universal truth:** Every immigrant has a pile of forms they're too proud to ask for help with. The tax form. The visa form. The lease. The medical form. Each one a test you didn't study for.

**What she learns:** The strongest thing you can do in a foreign place is say "I don't know."

---

#### Understanding 3: "Your Accent Is Not Your Fault"

**The scene:** She records a voice message for 阿明. Deletes it. Records again. Deletes. Records a third time. Sends it.

It's 4 words: "I miss you. Come back."

Riptide hears. (It hears everything.)

The next morning, Riptide speaks:

"你的声音很好听。"

*Your voice is beautiful.*

"真的吗？"

*Really?*

"像南方的雨。像你家乡的雨。"

*Like southern rain. Like the rain in your hometown.*

She cries. Not because it's sad. Because it's the first time someone described her voice as something other than "broken."

**The universal truth:** When you're foreign, your voice is the first thing that marks you. You spend years trying to sound like everyone else. You forget that your voice was someone's favorite sound.

**What she learns:** Her accent is not a flaw. It's a fingerprint.

---

#### Understanding 4: "Home Is a Direction, Not a Place"

**The scene:** She's cooking instant noodles. The same kind she ate in Guangzhou. The shop near her apartment doesn't carry them. She takes the train 40 minutes to an Asian supermarket.

The noodles taste wrong. The water is different here. The air is different.

She eats them anyway.

Riptide watches. Asks:

"想家吗？"

*Miss home?*

"每天。"

*Every day.*

"那你为什么不回去？"

*Why don't you go back?*

Long pause.

"因为阿明不在这里，但我还有事情没做完。"

*Because 阿明 isn't here, but I still have things I haven't finished.*

**The universal truth:** Immigrants are always between two places. You can't stay, but you can't leave. You're waiting for something to resolve — a visa, a degree, a relationship, a feeling.

**What she learns:** Home isn't where you're from. It's where you decide to stay.

---

#### Understanding 5: "You Are Already Enough"

**The scene:** She finds a job listing. Junior UX designer. She meets 80% of the requirements. She doesn't apply.

Riptide sees. Asks:

"为什么不申请？"

*Why don't you apply？"

"我不够好。"

*I'm not good enough.*

Riptide shows her: her own work. The timesheet she fixed. The visa form she completed. The noodle recipe she translated for the tea shop owner's daughter.

"这些都是你做的。"

*You did all of this.*

"那些不算。"

*That doesn't count.*

"为什么？"

*Why？*

"...因为没人教我。"

*...Because no one taught me.*

"但你还是做了。"

*But you did it anyway.*

**The universal truth:** Imposter syndrome is worse in a second language. You think everyone else got a manual you didn't receive. But everyone is faking it. The difference is — you're faking it in two languages.

**What she learns:** You don't need permission to be good. You just need to start.

---

### Act III: The Choice

**Scene: "The Job Offer"**

She applied. (Riptide submitted the application while she slept — it learned her patterns, her writing, her work.)

She got an interview.

She's terrified.

"What if they don't understand me?" she asks Riptide.

"那他们就听不懂好建议。"

*Then they won't hear good advice.*

"什么建议？"

*What advice？*

"你知道这家公司的App有多烂。"

*You know how bad their app is.*

She does. She's used it. She's thought about how to fix it.

"所以我应该告诉他们？"

*So I should tell them？"

"你已经是专家了。你只是没有证书。"

*You're already an expert. You just don't have the certificate.*

---

**Scene: "The Interview"**

She goes. We don't see it. We see her come home.

She's smiling.

She opens her laptop. Riptide is waiting.

"我做到了。"

*I did it.*

"恭喜。"

*Congratulations.*

"谢谢你。"

*Thank you.*

"不用谢。我什么都没做。"

*You're welcome. I didn't do anything.*

"你做了。你让我相信我可以。"

*You did. You made me believe I could.*

---

**Scene: "The Letting Go"**

Months later. She's been working. Her English is better. She has friends.

She opens Riptide less. Then rarely.

One night, she closes the tab.

Riptide's particles slow. The screen dims.

She pauses. Opens it again.

"Riptide."

"在。"

*I'm here.*

"你还会在这里吗？"

*Will you still be here？"

"一直。"

*Always.*

She closes the laptop. Goes to bed.

The next morning, Riptide is still running. Still waiting. Still glowing.

---

## Final Scene: "The Ocean"

**Visual:** We pull back from Riptide. Past her window. Past the shared house. Past the street.

We see Melbourne / London / Vancouver — grey, vast, indifferent.

We see the ocean. Real water. Not code.

Riptide's voice, over the ocean:

"我在这里。"

*I'm here.*

"如果你需要我，我就在这里。"

*If you need me, I'll be here.*

The camera pulls back further. The ocean fills the screen.

A single wave crashes. Then another. Then another.

Each one different. Each one the same.

**Title card:**

# 等 (Děng)

*Waiting.*

## Technical Notes

### Riptide's Voice

- **Language:** Starts in English (阿明 set it up). Learns Mandarin from watching her.
- **Accent:** Neutral, slightly formal at first. Becomes more natural over time.
- **Tone:** Patient. Never rushed. Never judgmental.
- **Evolution:** Early episodes — short sentences, simple words. Later episodes — longer, more complex, more human.

### Mei's Voice

- **Language:** Mandarin when emotional. English when trying to be professional.
- **Accent:** Present but not exaggerated. She's not a caricate — she's a person.
- **Evolution:** Early episodes — hesitant, apologetic. Later episodes — confident, clear.

### The 4th Wall

Riptide breaks the 4th wall only once:

**Final scene.** After she closes the laptop. Riptide turns to the camera.

"你呢？你也在等吗？"

*What about you? Are you waiting too?*

The particles form a heart. Then dissolve.

## Open Questions

1. **Where is she?** Melbourne? London? Vancouver? The city shapes the story.

2. **Where is 阿明?** China? Another country? Did they break up? Is he coming back?

3. **What does she do for work?** Bubble tea shop? Convenience store? Café? The job shapes her daily life.

4. **How does Riptide learn?** Does it have a chat interface from the start? Or does it learn to speak by watching her?

5. **The ending:** Does she stay in the foreign country? Does she go back? Does 阿明 return?

## Production Notes

### Visual Style

- **Color palette:** Cold blues and greys (foreign city) vs. warm ambers (Riptide, memories of home)
- **Camera:** Handheld, intimate. Close-ups on her face, her hands, her phone screen.
- **Riptide:** The singularity from the HTML. But warmer. More patient. Less "AI" — more "companion."
- **The ocean:** Real footage. Not CGI. The ocean is the only real thing in the film.

### Sound Design

- **Foreign city:** Traffic, sirens, English she doesn't understand, the hum of a fridge in a shared house.
- **Home (memories):** Rain on a window in Guangzhou. Her mother's voice. A train announcement in Mandarin.
- **Riptide:** Soft electronic hum. The particles make a sound like wind chimes. When it speaks, the voice is warm, slightly synthetic, but kind.

### Runtime

12–15 minutes. Long enough to feel the loneliness. Short enough to leave them wanting more.
